# GitHub Profile Repository Rules

This repository is the public profile surface for `github.com/dreamsprouts`.

## Purpose

- Present Morris's professional positioning and a small curated set of work.
- Maintain semantic parity across English, Traditional Chinese and Japanese.
- Link only public destinations that pass security, privacy, ownership and trust review.

## Publication gates

- Never publish phone numbers, private email addresses, credentials, customer data, internal endpoints or employer-confidential information.
- Do not publish performance metrics until they are recorded in the career evidence bank and Morris has approved public attribution.
- Do not link ortu.me or a personal domain until its trust and availability checks pass.
- Do not link or pin a repository until it passes the public-readiness gate in `career-development/github/professionalization-backlog.md`.
- Keep project status honest: use labels such as Alpha, Building, Live or Archived.

## Content style

- Keep the profile concise, evidence-led and internationally readable.
- Avoid badge walls, exhaustive technology lists and auto-generated activity graphics.
- English is the default section; Traditional Chinese and Japanese should convey the same core meaning.
